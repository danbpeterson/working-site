Preparing for Surgery

Good planning before surgery can make the experience more comfortable and help you recover with confidence.

Before Your Procedure

Review your medication list, follow any activity restrictions, and bring important questions to your pre-op visit.

On the Day of Surgery

Arrive on time, follow fasting instructions, and be ready to discuss your care plan with your surgical team.
