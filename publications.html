Publications and Research

Active research and publication allow orthopaedic surgeons to stay at the forefront of evolving techniques and evidence-based care, ensuring their patients benefit from the most current and effective treatments. It fosters collaboration and credibility within the medical community, advancing the field as a whole.

Dr. Peterson’s work spans total joint arthroplasty, unicompartmental knee arthroplasty, spinal deformity, and trauma outcomes, with a strong emphasis on value-based care and patient-reported outcomes. She has over 25 publications and is an active reviewer for various journals. Dr. Peterson's full body of work can be found through the OCRID website.
