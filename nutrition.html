Nutrition Tips

Nutrition plays an important role in healing, energy, and long-term health before and after surgery.

Before Surgery

Maintain a balanced diet, stay hydrated, and follow any specific instructions from your care team.

After Surgery

Prioritize protein, vegetables, and enough calories to support healing while staying mindful of your recovery plan.
