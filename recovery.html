What Recovery Looks Like

Recovery is a gradual process that varies by procedure, but steady progress and follow-through with therapy are essential.

Early Recovery

Expect pain management, mobility exercises, and close follow-up in the first few weeks after surgery.

Long-Term Healing

As strength improves, your care team will help you return to the activities that matter most to you.
